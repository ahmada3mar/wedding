Our Story

A year of searching, one unforgettable glance, and a love written in the stars — this is how we found our forever.

🕊️

The Long Search

For a whole year I searched, hoping to find the one my heart was waiting for. Face after face, no one ever felt right. Tired and almost ready to give up, I held on to a quiet belief that, somewhere, she was meant for me.

✨

The First Glance

💞 29 August 2025

And then, the moment fate had been saving arrived. From the very first look, she stole my heart. Without a second thought, I knew deep inside — she is the one. Her eyes, her smile, the warmth she carried, touched something in me I had never felt before.

💬

Falling in Love

What started with the first messages soon became long calls that stretched into the night. With every word and every shared laugh, I fell deeper — until loving her felt as natural as breathing.

🌹

The Engagement · الخطبة

🌹 12 December 2025

With the blessing of our families, the day of our Khotba arrived — the cherished moment when promises were spoken and hearts were joined. Surrounded by those we love most, I asked for her hand, and she became my fiancée, my future, and the answer to every prayer. It was the day our two families became one, and the sweet beginning of a lifetime together.

💍

My Whole World

From that day on, she became all of my life — my home, my peace, and my greatest love. And now we can't wait to celebrate the rest of our story, surrounded by everyone we hold dear.

🌙

Written in the Stars · One Year to the Day

💍 29 August 2026

On the 29th of August 2025, a single glance changed our lives forever. And on the 29th of August 2026 — exactly one year later, to the very day — we'll say "I do." What love began, time has blessed; the day we first met is the same day we'll begin forever. Some stories you write — ours, it seems, was written for us.
